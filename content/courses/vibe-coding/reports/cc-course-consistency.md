# Cross-lesson consistency check: cc-01 … cc-11 (2026-10-21)

Each lesson passed its own reviews; this check compared the 11 drafts with each other. Eight checkers (lenses: references between lessons, terminology, facts on permissions, context, skills and subagents, hooks/MCP/plugins, automation and cost, and course progression) read digests of all lessons and the lesson files. A separate skeptic then tried to refute every finding against the lesson files and the fact sources (`cross-lesson-facts.md`, the per-lesson audits, research report §2–§3). Only findings the skeptic could not refute were applied.

Result: 47 findings, 29 confirmed (18 distinct places, several lenses found the same problem), 18 refuted. All confirmed findings were fixed; the changed lessons were then re-reviewed (visual QA on a fresh preview, pedagogy-reviewer and ja-editor).
